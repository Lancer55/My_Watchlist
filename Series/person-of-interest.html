Synopsis

An reclusive billionaire computer programmer named Harold Finch develops a sophisticated artificial intelligence system for the U.S. government known as "The Machine," which analyzes mass surveillance data to predict acts of terrorism. However, the system also predicts premeditated violent crimes involving ordinary civilians—deemed "irrelevant" by the government. Finch recruits John Reese, a former CIA operative presumed dead, to use the Machine's cryptic social security numbers to intervene and prevent these crimes before they happen.
