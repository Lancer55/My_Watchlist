Synopsis

Based on the hit Naver Webtoon Manager Kim, the series follows Kim Do-hyeon, a quiet salaryman and single father living a low-profile life. However, behind his mild exterior lies his true past as "Agent 66," one of South Korea's most dangerous former covert operatives. When his high school daughter suddenly vanishes under dangerous circumstances, Kim abandons his peaceful civilian life and reactivates his lethal espionage skills to hunt down her captors and bring her home.
