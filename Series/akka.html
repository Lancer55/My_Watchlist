Synopsis

Set in the 1980s within the fictional matriarchal city of Pernuru in South India, Akka is a gritty period crime thriller produced by YRF Entertainment. Following the death of the ruling head, Bhavani steps up to command the clan. However, her authority faces a ruthless challenge from Rudra, a fearless outsider with deeply rooted ties to Pernuru who aims to dismantle the matriarchy and shift the balance of power.
