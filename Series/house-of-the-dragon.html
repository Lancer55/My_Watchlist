Synopsis

Set 200 years before the events of Game of Thrones, House of the Dragon tells the story of the House Targaryen at the height of its power and its eventual decline into a devastating civil war. Known as the "Dance of the Dragons," the succession conflict erupts between Princess Rhaenyra Targaryen and her half-brother Aegon II over who should inherit the Iron Throne following the death of King Viserys I, threatening to tear Westeros apart with dragonfire.
