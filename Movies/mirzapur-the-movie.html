Synopsis

Taking the brutal politics, relentless violence, and raw power struggles of the crime capital of Purvanchal from streaming to the big screen, Mirzapur: The Movie reunites iconic characters for an explosive cinematic chapter. As old rivalries reach a boiling point and new alliances form, the battle for control over the iron throne of Mirzapur scales up with higher stakes, larger-than-life action set pieces, and a definitive reckoning for the underworld empire.
