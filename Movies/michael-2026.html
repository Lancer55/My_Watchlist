Synopsis

Michael is a cinematic portrayal exploring the complex life and legacy of Michael Jackson, the King of Pop. The biographical drama charts his journey from a child prodigy leading the Jackson 5 to becoming the grandest entertainer in world history. Directed by Antoine Fuqua and starring Jackson's real-life nephew Jaafar Jackson in a highly praised performance, the film dives deep into both his boundary-pushing artistic process and the tumultuous personal life behind the stage.
