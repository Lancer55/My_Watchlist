Dil Bechara

"Janam kab lena hai aur marna kab hai, hum decide nahi kar sakte. Par kaise jeena hai, woh hum decide kar sakte hain."

Movie Info

  • Director: Mukesh Chhabra
  • Release Year: 2020
  • Genre: Romance / Drama / Comedy
  • IMDb Rating: 8.3/10

Synopsis

Based on John Green's bestselling novel The Fault in Our Stars, Dil Bechara follows Kizie Basu, a young woman battling thyroid cancer, and Immanuel "Manny" Rajkumar Junior, a spirited former osteosarcoma patient who refuses to let illness dim his zest for life. After meeting at a cancer support group, the two embark on a deeply moving, bittersweet journey of love, laughter, and fulfilling Kizie's dream of meeting her favorite musician in Paris.
