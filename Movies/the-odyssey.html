Synopsis

An epic mythic action-adventure adapting Homer's foundational ancient Greek poem, The Odyssey follows Odysseus, King of Ithaca, on his perilous ten-year voyage home following the fall of Troy. Facing wrathful gods, mythical monsters, and treacherous waters, Odysseus must rely on his wit and resilience to reunite with his wife Penelope and reclaim his kingdom.
